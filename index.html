The 36-Hour Cold Ferment Process

Every single loaf crafted at The Fermented Table undergoes a natural 36-hour cold ferment. We use organic, unbleached stoneground flours combined with our mother culture to create loaves with crisp caramelized crusts and open, airy crumbs.
